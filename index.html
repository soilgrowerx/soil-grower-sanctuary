Field Handbook: Sanctuary Zero

The Five Ecological Pillars

1. Vertical Soil Horizons (O, A, E, B, C): Regenerative arboriculture manages depth, not surface dirt. The O layer absorbs raindrop impact; the A topsoil hosts root-microbe symbiosis; the E layer is the leached zone; the B subsoil holds the deep clay and mineral phosphorus bank; and the C layer is the calcareous parent substrate.

2. Fungal-to-Bacterial (F:B) Succession: Compacted dirt is bacterial (< 0.4). High-lignin woodchips and aerated fungal teas shift succession toward forest ecosystems (> 3.0), unlocking climax canopy trees.

3. Mycorrhizal Hyphal Networks: Living fungal hyphae connect mature nurse trees to young saplings, shuttling photosynthate carbon to understory seedlings.

4. Infiltration versus Runoff: Kinetic raindrop energy shatters bare soil aggregates, forming an impervious surface crust that causes topsoil erosion. Coarse woodchip mulch and AirSpade macropores channel storm water deep into the B-horizon moisture bank.

5. Asynchronous Accountless Cooperation: Players share sanctuary field states via URL-encoded seeds and dispatch care packages across browsers without logins or accounts.

The Mars Horizon

Sanctuary Zero forges the Soil Grower Cooperative Standard on Earth. The exact biological equations used here to rebuild compacted clay into living rhizosphere apply directly to closed-loop soil genesis on Martian basaltic regolith.
