## 1. Game Overview & Elevator Pitch

*Soil Grower: Sanctuary Zero* is an ecological restoration simulator that models real rhizosphere physics and soil biology. Unlike conventional farming or garden games that treat soil as a flat 2D tilled surface, *Soil Grower* models soil depth across vertical horizons (O, A, E, B, and C). The player takes on the role of an ecological steward at Sanctuary Zero, transforming a degraded, compacted urban lot into a self-sustaining living sanctuary by managing fungal-to-bacterial succession, building water-stable aggregates, clearing root flare compaction with pneumatic AirSpade tools, and weaving living mycorrhizal networks.

## 2. Five Core Mechanics That No Comparable Game Implements

1. **Vertical Soil Horizons (Depth Physics):**
   The player manages depth across five distinct horizons: Organic mulch layer (O), biologically active topsoil root zone (A), eluviated mineral zone (E), illuvial subsoil clay bank holding deep moisture and mineral phosphorus (B), and calcareous parent substrate (C). Each horizon tracks bulk density, field capacity, water content, and aggregate stability.

2. **Fungal-to-Bacterial (F:B) Microbial Succession:**
   Soil succession dictates what vegetation can thrive. Degraded soil begins dominated by bacteria (F:B ratio 0.20), supporting only ruderal weeds. By applying high-lignin arborist woodchips and aerated fungal compost teas, players shift the F:B ratio upward past 1.2 (perennial guilds) and 3.0 (climax canopy trees such as Live Oak, Douglas-fir, and Pecan). The "Synthetic N-P-K Trap" demonstrates why chemical fertilizers destroy regenerative soil by spiking salinity and killing 45% of fungal biomass.

3. **Mycorrhizal Hyphal Network Carbon Flow:**
   Living mycorrhizal hyphae connect mature nurse trees to understory saplings. Carbon photosynthate generated in sunlit leaves travels through underground fungal channels to subsidize shaded seedlings. Cutting a nurse tree severs the hyphal pipeline and halts the carbon subsidy.

4. **Infiltration versus Runoff Hydrology:**
   Storms arrive with real rainfall intensity and raindrop kinetic energy. Bare, crusted soil shatters under raindrop impact, sealing surface macropores and producing lateral runoff that washes topsoil carbon off-plot. Mulched, decompacted soil absorbs raindrops, channeling 100% of storm pulses into the deep B-horizon water bank.

5. **Accountless Asynchronous Cooperation:**
   Players share and steward sanctuaries without logins, accounts, or friend lists. A single URL-encoded seed carries the entire field state. Visiting stewards can dispatch mulch and biological care packages to another player's sanctuary, leaving a permanent audit trace and boosting the community compounding multiplier.
