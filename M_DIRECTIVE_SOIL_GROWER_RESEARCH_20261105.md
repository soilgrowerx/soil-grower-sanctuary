## Why

A cooperative sanctuary-building game is the proposed pivot for Soil Grower LLC, and it enters the Base44 x Nitro contest, which closes 2026-12-03 17:59 Central. Before any spec is written, the market has to be mapped honestly, including whether the idea has room to be novel and what comparable games actually earn and retain.

## What to produce

**1. Comparable landscape.** Ten to fifteen games that occupy adjacent ground: cooperative farming and building sims, sanctuary or restoration games, games with a shared-stake or guild economy, and any web-native game that sustains a loop without an account. For each: platform, price model, rough audience size if published, core loop in one sentence, and what it does that a soil sanctuary game would have to match.

**2. The cooperative-economy question.** Is there an existing game where players, producers and landowners share proceeds, and how is it structured? This is the crux. Report what works, what was tried and abandoned, and what the legal or platform limits were. Mark anything you cannot verify.

**3. Audience and discovery.** Who plays this genre, how do they find games, and what does a browser game need to survive first-week retention. Cite storefront or platform data where it exists. No invented percentages.

**4. Competitive gap.** Where is the unfilled space. Be specific: what no existing game does that this one could.

**5. Failure modes.** Three to five ways games of this shape die, from scope to economy to asset cost, each with an example.

**6. Base44 platform reality.** What a browser game on Base44 can and cannot do today: save state, real-time, asset handling, performance ceiling, hosting limits, what the free tier allows and what paid tiers unlock. This is engineering intelligence, not marketing copy, and it should be sourced from their documentation.

## Constraints

- Every claim anchored to a source. Mark UNVERIFIED where you cannot reach one.
- No invented player counts, revenue figures, or retention percentages. If a number is not published, say so.
- Do not design the game. This is research, and design authority stays with George and Rocky.
