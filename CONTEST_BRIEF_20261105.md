## Two tracks, one game, two submissions

**Judging panel, $50,000.** Three criteria, equally weighted:

1. **Gameplay Quality.** How enjoyable, polished and replayable.
2. **Creativity.** Originality of concept, design and presentation.
3. **Technical Execution.** Performance, stability, responsiveness, quality of build.

Tie-break order: Gameplay Quality, then Technical Execution, then Creativity.

**Launchpad, $15,000.** The 25 submissions with the most valid upvotes go to a Base44 panel, which picks 11 winners. One upvote per natural person per submission. Bots, alts, vote swaps, paid votes and incentives are audited and can disqualify the game itself.

The same game can win both, but it must be submitted to each track separately.

## Hard requirements that shape the design

- Browser-based, playable in a current desktop or mobile browser, with no download, install, plug-in or extension.
- **Free to access and play in full.** No login, no registration, no subscription, no payment, no paywall, no in-game purchase, and no other condition to play.
- Live and publicly accessible at the submitted URL from submission through judging, and for at least 90 days after the winner announcement.
- No third-party intellectual property of any kind without a written licence or full compliance with an open licence. That covers artwork, music, sound, characters, names, logos, assets.
- No gambling, wagering or lotteries for money or anything of value.
- Must not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.
- Must be the entrant's own original work created during the submission period.
- Overwolf may demand access to the Base44 project and its edit history as proof.

## Intellectual property

**The entrant keeps ownership.** Section 11.1 states plainly that entering does not transfer ownership to Overwolf or Base44.

In exchange, the entrant grants Overwolf and Base44 a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, transferable and sublicensable licence to host, reproduce, display, promote and adapt the submission for contest administration and promotion. That is a promotional licence, not an assignment.

Practical reading for Soil Grower: the game concept, the world, and the cooperative economy stay George's. The contest can show it forever. Any future commercial version is unaffected, but the contest build itself is licensed for their promotion.

## Teams

Teams of up to four natural persons, each eligible, with one representative who submits and receives any prize. For this project the meaningful consequence is that helpers cannot be added casually: every member must hold their own Base44 account in good standing.

## What the contest rewards, read against the criteria

The panel scores equally, so a beautiful, technically clean game with a thin loop loses to a competent game with a loop people replay. Polish and stability carry a third of the score on their own, which means scope discipline is worth more than feature count.

The Launchpad is a popularity contest with anti-manipulation enforcement. Real distribution beats clever campaigning, and manufactured votes risk the whole submission.

## Risks to plan around

**Scope.** Four weeks, one builder plus agents. A cooperative economy sim is a large idea. The judging panel rewards a complete small loop over an incomplete grand one.

**Free-to-play with no account.** All progression must live somewhere that is not a login: local storage, a shareable seed, or nothing. That decision constrains saves, leaderboards and any social feature.

**The real-money idea cannot ship here.** A game where players and landowners receive actual shares of real production is a securities and gambling problem, and the terms forbid wagering for value. The contest build can model the economy honestly in game currency, with the real-world cooperative version as the product that comes later.

**Assets.** Every sprite, sound and font needs a licence that permits commercial-adjacent promotional use. Generated assets need to be reproducible and owned, which means a record of the generation source.

## Submission mechanics

1. Build on Base44, hosted there.
2. Submit to the main competition at nitropay.com/base44webgame.
3. Submit separately through the Base44 Launchpad.
4. Keep the URL live for 90 days after winners are announced.
