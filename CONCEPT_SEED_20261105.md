# Soil Grower: concept seed

Recorded 2026-11-05 from George, before any design work. This is the source of intent, not a spec.

## The idea in his words

A "Soil Grower" game focused on sanctuary building. Real physical work and production connect to a game level where kids get to command game activity and Soil Grower activity. There is an economic layer where the soil grower, the land or property owner, and the players each get a share. The game is built in the spirit of a cooperative.

He has wanted this for years.

## Why this contest

The Base44 platform removes the engine problem: browser game, no download, hosted, prompt-driven build with frontier models available. If the contest build lands, the plan is to convert Soil Grower LLC toward this: games and media around the cooperative economy, stepping away from AirSpade work, with STIM Biosciences LLC funded by a network of Soil Growers.

That is the strategic frame. The contest is the forcing function, not the destination.

## The thing that makes it not a farm sim

Most farm games simulate agriculture. This one proposes that the people who do the physical work, the people who own the land, and the people who play all hold a stake in what the land produces. Sanctuary building is the verb: the land gets better, not just fuller.

The honest version of that idea in a four week contest build: model the cooperative in game currency, with real production numbers that a grower would recognize. The real-money version is a later product with real legal structure. Do not blur them.

## Design constraints that follow

**Free, no login, no paywall.** Contest rule. So a player's progress lives in local storage or a shareable seed, not an account.

**No gambling.** Shares, stakes and yields must not read as wagering for value.

**Children will play it.** No data collection from under 13, and no design that requires an adult to hand over personal information to play.

**One loop, finished.** Four weeks. The panel scores gameplay, creativity and technical execution equally, so a complete small loop beats an unfinished grand one.

**Assets must be ours.** Generated or original, with a record of provenance.

## What must be decided before Rocky plans

1. **The verb.** Is the core loop planting and tending, designing a sanctuary layout, or directing a crew? Pick one as the moment-to-moment action.
2. **The cooperative.** Is the share model a visible mechanic the player manages, or background flavor that surfaces in a season summary?
3. **The tie to real work.** Does the game read live data from a real grower operation, or use a modeled operation that a grower would recognize as true?
4. **Scope.** Sanctuary building as a sandbox, or as a progression with seasons and constraints.

Answer those and the spec writes itself. Skip them and the build becomes a museum of good intentions.
